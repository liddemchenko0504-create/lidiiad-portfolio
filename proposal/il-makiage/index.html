02 / SEARCH FOUNDATION

START WITH
THE BASICS.
MAKE THEM BETTER.

Before investing in experimental AI visibility tactics, I would examine where technical SEO and on-page product storytelling can be strengthened using data from crawls, search performance and customer intent.

Priority 01 / PDP & category content

From product listings to informed decisions.

Build a clear relationship between non-branded search queries, benefit-led category copy, product details and decision-support content.

  • Group queries by coverage, finish, skin needs and shopping intent.
  • Map each intent cluster to the most appropriate URL.
  • Review titles, descriptions, headings, on-page copy and internal links.
  • Identify where comparison content or FAQs help real customers.
TECHNICAL REVIEW

Indexation & crawl paths

Inspect robots rules, sitemap coverage, indexability, redirects, canonicals, duplicate URLs, structured data and internal linking. Validate fixes with developers rather than making assumptions from audit tools alone.

INTERNATIONAL READINESS

Localization without drift

Audit regional URLs, hreflang, market-specific availability and language consistency so users find accurate local pages rather than conflicting product information.

03 / FROM KEYWORDS TO QUESTIONS

SEARCH HAS
MORE CONTEXT
NOW.

A keyword often describes a category. A conversational request can describe an entire decision. Product content should support both—without inventing benefits or promising that AI assistants will recommend a specific brand.

Traditional search
“Natural finish foundation”

Focus: a concise keyword, search result relevance and an appropriate destination page.

Conversational discovery
“Which foundation might work for combination skin with buildable coverage and a natural finish?”

Focus: specific product evidence, transparent limitations and a useful comparison.

“The goal is not to force a product into an AI answer. It is to make approved, crawlable product information accurate and useful when the product is genuinely relevant.”

04 / POWERMATCH & PERSONALIZATION

TURN CUSTOMER
QUESTIONS INTO
BETTER CONTENT.

PowerMatch shows how a guided journey can reduce uncertainty in beauty shopping. My proposal is to use learnings from approved quiz journeys and product attributes to inform content strategy and experimentation—not to expose individual quiz responses or mix personalization with marketing consent.

A connected content model

Customer intent → product evidence.

  • Document recurring decision points: shade, undertone, coverage, finish and preferences.
  • Compare approved product attributes with existing PDP explanations.
  • Spot recurring questions that need better guides or clearer content.
  • Test changes through defined CRO experiments and review outcomes.

Any use of customer-level data would require appropriate permissions, privacy review and approved access controls.

WORKFLOW.

I would begin with internal, low-risk use cases, where AI can accelerate repetitive work and specialists remain responsible for accuracy, final copy and publication.

Deliverable: reviewed keyword-to-URL map
Content operations

PDP copy support

Draft structured descriptions from verified attributes, with guardrails against fabricated claims.

International markets

Localization QA

Compare language variants for missing product details, mismatched claims and formatting issues.

Guardrails: no unapproved customer data in external AI tools, no invented product claims, no auto-publishing without human review, and no assumption that using an LLM improves SEO rankings by itself.

06 / HOW I WOULD START

SHOW THE WORK.
TEST THE IDEA.

Rather than proposing a large transformation before seeing current priorities, I would start with a focused sample of pages and one reviewable AI workflow, then recommend next steps based on the findings.

STEP 01 / BASELINE

Review a representative page set.

Use GSC, GA4 and a targeted crawl to identify technical issues, search-intent gaps and existing opportunities. Choose sample pages with the team.

STEP 02 / VISUAL RECOMMENDATIONS

Show concrete PDP improvements.

Prepare before/after examples for metadata, content structure, product attributes and internal linking, with clear developer/content requirements.

STEP 03 / ONE LLM PILOT

Prototype an internal workflow.

Build a supervised process such as keyword clustering or approved attribute-to-copy drafting. Compare output quality and review effort to the existing workflow.

STEP 04 / EVALUATION

Measure what actually changed.

Track technical fixes, content quality, relevant impressions/clicks, workflow time, editorial errors and follow-on tests. Report limitations and avoid claiming causation without evidence.

WHAT WE CAN MEASURE

Organic visibility

Relevant queries, impressions and clicks.

Page quality

Indexability, structured data, content completeness.

Product relevance

Match between user intent and verified product details.

Workflow quality

Review time, corrections and reusable outputs.

These are proposed evaluation signals, not reported business outcomes or guarantees.
