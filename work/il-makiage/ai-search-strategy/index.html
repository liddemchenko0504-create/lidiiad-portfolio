IL MAKIAGE / INDEPENDENT STRATEGY CONCEPT / AI DISCOVERY

STRATEGY FOR A BEAUTY BRAND.

From traditional search results to conversational product recommendations. A framework for making beauty products easier for AI-powered search systems to discover, interpret and recommend when relevant.

01 / The opportunity

BEAUTY DISCOVERY IS CHANGING.

A customer no longer needs to search for a product by name. They can describe their skin, expectations and preferences, then ask an AI assistant to compare what might suit them.

For a beauty brand, that changes the task. Traditional SEO helps people find pages through search results. AI search visibility adds another layer: making information about a brand and its products legible enough to be found, retrieved, evaluated and accurately represented in an AI-generated answer.

For IL MAKIAGE, I would explore how its foundation and complexion portfolio, shade-matching experience, product education and supporting editorial content could participate in these new discovery journeys. This is a proposed strategy, not a claim that these optimizations have been implemented by the brand.

THE OBJECTIVE IS NOT TO FORCE A RECOMMENDATION. IT IS TO MAKE A PRODUCT EASY TO UNDERSTAND — AND RELEVANT WHEN IT ACTUALLY FITS.
02 / The customer journey

FROM KEYWORDS TO QUESTIONS.

Consider the difference between a conventional search phrase and a conversational request. The latter contains more context, and good product content should help answer it accurately.

TRADITIONAL SEARCH

“Medium coverage foundation”

CONVERSATIONAL DISCOVERY

“What foundation might suit combination skin, with buildable coverage, a natural finish and a good shade match?”

This shift suggests a content model built around skin concerns, undertones, finishes, usage and comparisons — without replacing essential product facts with generic AI-written copy.

03 / The strategy

FOUR CONNECTED WORKSTREAMS.

01 / PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

CONTENT THAT ANSWERS REAL QUESTIONS.

Develop detailed, evidence-based attributes for each product: coverage, finish, formula claims, usage, available shades, undertones, ingredient details and limitations. Align product pages, category pages and educational content around specific shopping needs. Keep approved claims consistent across all markets.

02 / TECHNICAL READINESS

CRAWLABLE AND CONSISTENT PRODUCT DATA.

Review indexing controls, canonical URLs, internal links and sitemaps. Validate supported Product, Offer, BreadcrumbList and Organization structured data where appropriate. Keep prices, availability and product identifiers aligned between the storefront and any merchant feeds. Structured data helps interpretation but does not guarantee AI citations.

03 / BRAND AUTHORITY

CREDIBILITY BEYOND A PRODUCT PAGE.

Strengthen useful makeup guides, expert-reviewed educational content, genuine product reviews, credible editorial coverage and consistent brand references. Build context around product benefits and intended users without manufacturing reviews, overstating endorsements or treating paid mentions as independent authority.

04 / INTERNATIONAL DISCOVERY

ACCURACY ACROSS MARKETS.

Audit localized product information, language versions, hreflang, regional URLs and availability. A recommendation should direct shoppers to the right market and show relevant, current information rather than relying on an outdated or geographically inappropriate page.

04 / Execution

HOW I WOULD APPROACH IT.

A practical optimization project starts with evidence, not claims of success.

  1. Establish a baseline.Build a fixed collection of non-branded beauty-shopping prompts. Record which products are mentioned, which sources are cited and whether descriptions are accurate. Include competing brands to avoid brand-biased testing.
  2. Audit representative product and category pages.Use a technical crawl and inspect schema, canonical tags, indexability, content gaps, page relationships and localization. Compare crawlable content with what shoppers actually see.
  3. Map conversational intent to product evidence.Group questions by coverage, finish, skin type, undertone, application and concerns. Connect each group to an appropriate page or explain where a new guide would add value.
  4. Develop prioritized content and technical recommendations.Propose improved product attributes, comparison content, relevant FAQs, internal linking and data consistency. Follow actual platform documentation and validate changes before release.
  5. Repeat the test and report limitations.Retest the same prompts over time, record model and date, monitor citations and referral data, and acknowledge response variability. Separate observed changes from causation.
05 / Measurement

WHAT SUCCESS WOULD LOOK LIKE.

SignalHow I would assess it
Brand mentionsShare of relevant prompts in which IL MAKIAGE is mentioned, using a fixed test set and documented model versions.
Product relevanceWhether a mentioned product actually fits the described skin needs, coverage preferences and market.
Source visibilityAppearance of official product and educational pages among cited sources where the AI product supports citations.
Answer accuracyCorrectness of product names, formula attributes, shade information, availability and links.
Referral performanceIdentifiable AI referral sessions and on-site conversions where analytics allow attribution.
Technical qualityIndexability, supported structured data, feed consistency, internal linking and localized page hygiene.

These are proposed KPIs. No results, rankings, traffic uplift or implementation outcomes are asserted here.

07 / Framework & references

BUILT ON SEARCH FUNDAMENTALS.

AI discovery is not a replacement for technical SEO, merchandising quality or genuine brand trust. The strategy draws on established practices for crawl access, people-first content, structured commerce information and transparent source evaluation.
